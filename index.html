Questions agency owners ask

Where are your setters based?

Our setters work from Pakistan, where there's a large pool of people experienced on US campaigns and US night shifts. You hear every setter's voice sample before choosing, and they work fully on US hours.

Will seniors understand their accent?

That's exactly why you get voice samples and an interview first. We screen for clear, warm English. If your leads struggle with a setter during the trial, we replace them at no cost.

What about TCPA and DNC compliance?

Setters only call the lists you provide, follow your scripts, and respect your Do Not Call and consent rules. Cold-calling lists must be scrubbed against the National Do Not Call Registry and your internal DNC list. No robocalls, no AI voices, no outside lists. You stay in full control of who gets called.

Do I need my own leads and dialer?

You'll need your own leads. Many agencies also have aged leads that were never fully worked, which is a great place to start. If you already have a dialer, your setter works inside it. If you don't, we can set one up for you through our dialer partner, DataDialerVoip.

How do I know they're actually working?

Time tracking, call recordings, daily reports, and the activity in your own CRM. If anything looks off, you'll see it in days, not months, and we'll fix it.

Is there a contract?

Month-to-month, with 30 days' notice to cancel. There's no recruitment fee. You start with a short paid trial so you can be sure before committing.

How do payments work?

We invoice monthly in advance through PayPal. Stablecoin payment (USDC/USDT) is also available, with a small discount.
